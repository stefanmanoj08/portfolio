Approach

How I connect the dots

More than any single project, my work is driven by how I tackle ambiguous spaces—stripping away the noise to find the core signal. I don't just research product teams; I think like a product person. That means taking shared ownership of business metrics, marketing strategy, and technical realities so stakeholders become active research partners. I move comfortably between working with engineers and aligning with VPs, always holding two priorities in balance: creating durable, long-term frameworks while staying ready to solve the urgent, time-sensitive problems that keep decisions moving.
